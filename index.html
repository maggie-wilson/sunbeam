About Us

Hello! Our names are Maggie Wilson and Olivia Cieri, and we want to address the stress that comes with applying to college. Between not knowing if you'll like the people and if the experience aligns with what you're looking for. We are new to coding, and we created this website at Sunbeam, a coding initiative that inspires young women and girls to become more involved with coding. We are currently seniors and know how daunting the decsions that come with life after highschool can feel. With this website, you'll be able to find undergraduate students at the colleges you are interested in and veiw thier stats and bios, and chose to schedule a meeting with them. If you are an undergrad student and are hoping to inspire and help highschoolers you can submit your information and schedule meetings once contact information is exchanged.
